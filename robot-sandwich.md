# Peanut Butter Jelly Sandwich

## Assumptions
- 2 slices of bread  
- 1 jar of jelly  
- 1 jar of peanut butter  
- 1 clean butter knife  
- 1 clean spoon  
- 1 clean plate  

## Instructions
1. Place one slice of bread on the clean plate.  
2. Pick up the jelly jar, twist the lid counterclockwise to open it, and set the lid aside.  
3. Use the clean spoon to scoop jelly from the open jelly jar and spread it onto the face-up side of the bread on the plate.  
4. Pick up the jelly jar lid and twist it clockwise to close the jar.  
5. Place the second slice of bread on the plate next to the jelly-covered slice.  
6. Pick up the peanut butter jar, twist the lid counterclockwise to open it, and set the lid aside.  
7. Use the clean butter knife to scoop peanut butter from the open jar and spread it onto the face-up side of the second slice of bread.  
8. Pick up the peanut butter jar lid and twist it clockwise to close the jar.  
9. Place the slice of bread with peanut butter on top of the slice with jelly, peanut butter side facing down.

## Done When
- Both slices of bread are combined with the peanut butter and jelly sides touching.
